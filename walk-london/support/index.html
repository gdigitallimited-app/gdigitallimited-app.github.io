Help with recording, your map and keeping your walks.

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Walk London for iPhone

Send a short description of the problem. There is no sign-in or subscription to manage.

Contact support

Email g.digital.limited@gmail.com for help with Walk London. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Screenshots can help; check them for personal information before sending. The app is developed by George Francis.

Recording a walk

Tap the large walking button in the centre of the tab bar to start recording. Grant location permission and enable Precise Location when asked. While recording, the button lets you finish and save the walk. Recording can continue with the screen locked. If the app is force quit, recording cannot continue; reopen it to resume an unfinished walk.

Location or progress looks wrong

Check iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Walk London. Allow location access and enable Precise Location. GPS can be less accurate beside tall buildings, on parallel streets or indoors. Progress uses a fixed London street network and can differ from the distance you walked. Walks outside that network can still add recorded distance without adding London street coverage.

Maps and connection

Apple Maps needs a connection for map content that is not already cached. Recording and matching against the bundled street network can work offline. Map imagery and geographic data are provided by their respective sources; the app’s About section includes credits.

Keeping or moving your walks

Open Profile to export a JSON backup or restore a saved backup. An export includes recorded route positions and timestamps, so keep it somewhere you trust. Restore replaces current on-device data rather than merging it and requires you to finish an active walk first. Keep an export of your current data if you want a separate copy before restoring.

Before deleting the app

Walk London does not have an account service or automatic cross-device sync. Deleting the app removes its local walks and progress. Export a backup first if you want to keep a separate copy. Device backups and files you exported elsewhere are managed separately through your system or destination service.
