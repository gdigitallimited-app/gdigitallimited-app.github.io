Support

A little room for your thoughts.

Contact

Email g.digital.limited@gmail.com for help or feedback. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Mini Typewriter version and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords or private writing unless you choose to share a short example needed to explain a problem.

Create and find writing

Open Your writing to browse notes or projects. New creates a note. Under Projects, use Create to name a project and gather related writing. Search by title or text, and tap a document to open it. Edit the title directly on the writing paper.

Saving and keeping copies

Writing saves automatically on this device. There is no Mini Typewriter account or built-in cloud sync. Tap Share at the top of the paper to send the document's title and text to another app or keep a copy using an available service. Keep copies of important work before changing devices or removing the app.

Sound and realistic typing

Open Your writing, then Settings. Switch Sound on or off separately from Realistic Typewriter Mode. Realistic mode enables carriage movement and line-feed animation. Haptics depend on device support. Reading mode hides the keyboard and presents your writing as pages; iPad supports landscape spreads. Write returns to the editor.

Delete a document

Open Your writing and use the document’s Delete action, then confirm. Deletion removes the document from this device; it does not delete copies you shared or backed up.

If saving needs attention

If you see a saving warning, try Retry saving. Use Share to keep a copy of any writing you can open before troubleshooting. Check available device storage. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first step because its writing is stored locally. If the problem continues, contact support with the warning text.
