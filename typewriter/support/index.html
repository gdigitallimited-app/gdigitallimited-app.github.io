Support

A little room for your thoughts.

Contact

Email g.digital.limited@gmail.com for help or feedback. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Typewriter version and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords or private writing unless you choose to share a short example needed to explain a problem.

Create and find writing

Tap the writing library button at the top left of the paper. Tap + and choose a note, letter or manuscript. Search by title or text and use the filters to browse each type. Tap a document to open it. Tap its title on the paper to rename it.

Saving and keeping copies

Writing saves automatically on this device. There is no Typewriter account or built-in cloud sync. Tap Share at the top of the paper to send the document's title and text to another app or keep a copy using an available service. Keep copies of important work before changing devices or removing the app.

Change sounds, haptics and appearance

Open the sliders button at the top right of the paper. Choose Midnight, Parchment or Forest, adjust the type size, or switch mechanical sounds and key haptics on or off. Haptics depend on your device. Book keeps the paper above the keys; Automatic also adapts to compact landscape screens.

Delete a document

Open the library. Swipe a document to reveal Delete, or touch and hold it and choose Delete writing, then confirm. This removes the document from this device. It does not delete copies you shared or backed up.

If saving needs attention

If you see a saving warning, try Retry saving. Use Share to keep a copy of any writing you can open before troubleshooting. Check available device storage. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first step because its writing is stored locally. If the problem continues, contact support with the warning text.
