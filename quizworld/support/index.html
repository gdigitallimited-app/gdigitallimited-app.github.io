Support

A little help for your next discovery.

Contact

Email g.digital.limited@gmail.com for help with QuizWorld or to report a question. Include your device, app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

Restore purchases

Open Profile → Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restore Purchases is also available on the upgrade screens.

Question packs

Easy questions are free in every category. Medium, Hard and Expert are separate one-time purchases. Each unlocks that difficulty across all categories. Purchasing Expert does not include Medium or Hard.

Your scores

Your personal bests are saved on your device and shown in Profile. Profile → Settings → Reset Local Progress clears saved quiz results and coins without revoking purchases.

Report a question

Include the category, difficulty, question text and the answer you believe is correct.

Privacy

Read the QuizWorld privacy policy for information about local scores, purchases and support enquiries.
