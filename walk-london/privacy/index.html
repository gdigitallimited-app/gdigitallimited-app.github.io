Walk London

Privacy Policy

Your walks stay on your device. You choose when to share them.

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Walk London for iPhone

At a glance

Your recorded walks stay on your device. No account, advertising or developer-operated analytics. You choose when to export or share your routes.

Who operates Walk London

Walk London is developed by George Francis. For support or privacy enquiries, email g.digital.limited@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Walk London stores the GPS positions and timestamps of walks you explicitly start, along with walk distance, duration, visited boroughs and street-coverage progress. It also stores your distance-unit and haptic preferences. These records stay in the app’s local storage. The app does not require an account or send your saved walk history to a developer-operated server. Cross-device synchronization is not included.

Location access

The app asks for location access when you start a walk. Precise Location helps match your walk to London’s streets. An explicitly started walk can continue while the app is in the background or the screen is locked, with the system location indicator visible. Finishing the walk stops route recording. iOS controls location access, and you can change or revoke permission in iPhone Settings. Opening a map can display your current location if you have granted permission, without starting a saved walk.

Apple Maps and Location Services

Walk London uses Apple’s MapKit to display maps. Apple may process device, network and location information needed to provide map content and authorised location features. Apple handles its services under its own Privacy Policy and Apple Maps privacy information. The app’s developer does not receive a separate copy of your saved routes from Apple Maps.

Backups and sharing

If you choose Export backup, Walk London creates a JSON file containing your saved walks, recorded route positions and timestamps, street progress, preferences and any unfinished walk. You choose where to save or share it using the iOS document interface. The destination or service you choose handles that copy under its own terms and privacy practices. Restoring a backup replaces the data in the app rather than merging it, and a previous local copy is retained for recovery. Exported files are separate from the app’s local storage.

Retention and deletion

Walks and coverage remain in the app until its data is replaced by a restore or the app is deleted. A recovery copy kept before a restore also remains in local app storage. Deleting the app removes its on-device app storage, including local recovery copies. iOS or iCloud device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Files you exported, copies you shared and system backups are not deleted merely by deleting the app; manage those copies separately through their destination or system settings. We cannot retrieve or erase your local walks remotely because we do not hold them on a developer server.

Analytics, advertising and purchases

The current app includes no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, purchases or developer-operated account service. We do not sell your walk information. Apple may make sales information and diagnostics available through its developer services, depending on Apple’s services and your settings.

Support enquiries

If you contact g.digital.limited@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and attachments. We use it to answer your enquiry and investigate the reported problem. Only send route exports or screenshots if you intend to share the information they contain. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Correspondence may be processed by the email service used for your enquiry and is retained only as needed to provide support and meet applicable legal obligations.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or embedded tracking to it. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when serving GitHub Pages and handles that information under its Privacy Statement. Visiting an external link is subject to that destination’s privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

You control location permission, exports and device backups. Depending on your location, you may also have rights concerning personal information you provide directly to us, such as in a support enquiry. Contact us about access, correction, deletion or other requests. You may contact your local data-protection authority; in the UK, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office. Contact Apple or GitHub about information they handle independently.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its privacy practices change. The update date will appear on this page. Contact g.digital.limited@gmail.com with questions about Walk London or this policy.
